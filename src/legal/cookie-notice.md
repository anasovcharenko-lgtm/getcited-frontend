# Cookie Notice

**Last updated: 30 September 2026**

This notice explains the cookies and similar technologies GetCited uses. It sits
alongside our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and is written to meet the Privacy and
Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and the UK GDPR.

---

## The short version

We use cookies to keep you signed in. That is the only category we set without
asking. If we later add analytics, we will ask first, and you can say no without
losing anything.

---

## 1. What a cookie is

A small text file a website stores in your browser. It lets the site recognise
your browser on the next request. We also use `localStorage`, which works the
same way for our purposes and is covered by the same rules.

---

## 2. Cookies we set

### Strictly necessary — no consent required

These make the site work. Turn them off and you cannot stay signed in.

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sb-<project>-auth-token` | Supabase | Keeps you signed in between page loads | 1 hour, refreshed while you are active |
| `sb-<project>-auth-token-code-verifier` | Supabase | Security check during Google sign-in (PKCE) | End of sign-in |
| `getcited-lang` | GetCited | Remembers whether you chose English or Russian | 1 year |

PECR allows these without consent because you cannot use the service without
them.

### Analytics — only with your consent

**We do not currently use analytics cookies.** If we add them, a banner will
appear and nothing will be set until you accept. This table will be filled in at
that point.

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | — | — | — |

### Advertising

**None.** We do not run ads, we do not use advertising pixels, and we have no
plans to.

---

## 3. Third-party cookies

**Google** may set cookies on its own domain while you complete sign-in. Those
are governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours, and we cannot read them.

We do not embed YouTube, Facebook, Intercom, Hotjar or similar third-party
widgets that would set cookies on our pages.

---

## 4. Controlling cookies

Because we only set strictly necessary cookies today, there is no consent banner
to interact with. You can still clear or block cookies in your browser:

- **Chrome** — Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
- **Safari** — Settings → Privacy
- **Firefox** — Settings → Privacy & Security
- **Edge** — Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Blocking our cookies will sign you out and keep you signed out.

If we introduce analytics, you will be able to change your choice at any time
from a link in the site footer.

---

## 5. Do Not Track

Browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal inconsistently and there is no agreed
standard for responding to it, so we do not act on it. Since we set no tracking
cookies, there is nothing for it to switch off.
